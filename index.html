Card Cutter

Drop in a card scan and see it cut into a shadow box: thin sheets of card, stacked with small gaps, the monster layered by depth.

Working…

Drag the card to turn it. Works with scans of modern-frame cards. Your image never leaves your browser; the first cut downloads a 27 MB depth model, kept for next time.

Depth by Depth Anything V2 Small (Apache-2.0). A fan-made toy, not affiliated with or endorsed by Konami.
