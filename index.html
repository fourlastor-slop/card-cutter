Card Cutter

Drop in a card scan and see it cut into a shadow box: thin sheets of card, stacked with small gaps, the art layered by depth. Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic and Pokémon frames.

Working…

Drag the card to turn it. Works with scans of modern-frame cards: Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic (the frame since 2015) and Pokémon (Scarlet & Violet, full art too). A photo works if the card stands out from what it lies on. Your image never leaves your browser; the first cut downloads a 27 MB depth model, kept for next time.

Depth by Depth Anything V2 Small (Apache-2.0). A fan-made toy, not affiliated with or endorsed by Konami, Wizards of the Coast or The Pokémon Company.
